The gap many families face

A loved one has passed, or an annual observance is approaching. Relatives are in different cities or countries. Details of the family tradition are unclear. Lists online feel generic — or overwhelming.

DharmaSetu is a thin, honest layer of guidance: we help you map what typically comes next for your situation, then hand you to a priest who can confirm what applies to your family.

How it works

Three calm steps. No apps. No checkout.

Tradition-aware — not a booking catalogue

Generic puja marketplaces list services. DharmaSetu starts with understanding: a tradition-aware roadmap for your family, then a verified priest match. You stay in control; the priest confirms what applies.

Important: Rituals and observances vary by family, region, and sampradaya. Any roadmap from DharmaSetu must be confirmed with an appropriate priest before you act on it.

What we don't do

Clarity about scope matters as much as what we offer.

  • We do not claim spiritual outcomes (moksha, peace for ancestors, or similar).
  • We do not run maps, multi-city finders, or a full funeral operations desk.
  • We do not take payment for rites or run booking checkouts on this site.
  • We do not replace your family's elders, community, or chosen priest.
  • Annual reminders are planned; they are not built yet.
