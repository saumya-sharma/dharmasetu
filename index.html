The ordinary gap

Distance, knowledge, bandwidth

An observance is approaching — or a first set of rites needs planning. Relatives are in different cities or countries. Details of the family custom sit with someone you cannot easily reach. Online lists feel generic, or endless.

  • Distance You may be abroad or in another city while the rite is expected at home — or the reverse.
  • Knowledge Gotra, tithi, sampradaya, who leads — fragments exist, but a clear sequence does not.
  • Bandwidth Work, travel, and family logistics leave little room to research what “usually comes next.”

DharmaSetu is a thin, honest layer: orientation first, then a priest who knows your tradition. We do not replace elders or your community.

Continuity

The practice continues — the setting changes

Ancestral observances were shaped in courtyards and hometowns. Many families now hold them from apartments, other cities, or abroad. Both contexts deserve dignity; the rite is the bridge.

Trust

What we never claim

Clarity about scope matters as much as what we offer. Practices vary by family, region, and sampradaya — confirm with an appropriate priest.

  • We do not claim spiritual outcomes for any rite or observance.
  • We do not run maps, multi-city finders, or a funeral operations desk.
  • We do not take payment for rites or run booking checkouts here.
  • We do not replace your family’s elders, community, or chosen priest.

Any roadmap from DharmaSetu is a draft for conversation. A verified priest confirms what applies to your lineage before you act on it.
