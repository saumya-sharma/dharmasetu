Choghadiya vs Shastric Muhurat

While Choghadiya divides daylight into eight parts for immediate day-to-day journeys, authentic Shubh Muhurat relies on all five limbs (Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana) alongside Lagna Shuddhi for life-defining milestones.
