menu_book Authentic Shastra Dossier Sanskar #05

Namkaran (नामकरण संस्कार)

Sacred Naming & Cosmic Acoustic Resonance

Essential Vidhi Parameters

When it is done: 11th or 12th day, or Shukla Paksha muhurat
Who performs it: Parents, Mama (Maternal Uncle), Acharya
Duration: ~90 min tranquil havan & Vedic chanting
Lineage Canon: Paraskara Grihya Sutra
info Typical shastra priest dakshina honorarium range: ₹3,100 – ₹5,100 (guided directly by regional sampradaya traditions, never negotiated or haggled).

Step-by-Step Vidhi Flow

4 Key Phases
1

Shuddhi & Ganapati Puja

Sanctification of the baby, mother and prayer to remove hurdles.

2

Ghee & Honey Whispering

Father whispers the sacred Vedic syllable softly into the child’s right ear.

3

Four Names Revelation

Nakshatra name, Ishta Deity name, Month name, and worldly Calling name.

4

Elder Ashirwad & Cradle Geet

Touching the cradle with unbroken akshat, followed by women's blessings.

Essential Purity Samagri

Kansa bronze bowl Pure forest honey Desi cow ghee Unbroken Akshat Fresh Mango leaves Silver coin Gangajal
