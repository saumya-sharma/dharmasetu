The Philosophy of Vedic Rhythms (काल गणना)

In Vedic thought, time is not linear decay but a revolving cosmic wheel (Kala Chakra). Celebrating each tithi harmonizes our prana with universal pulsation. During Pitru Paksha, the veil thins between generations, allowing sons and daughters to express profound gratitude without despair.
