Educational overview

NRI continuity

Tradition variation: Diaspora temples, local priests, and home adaptations differ by country and community. What relatives in India expect may not match what is practical abroad — a priest who understands both contexts helps.

Distance is one of the main reasons annual care feels hard: Shraddha, Pitra Paksha, or a remembrance yatra is due in India while you are in the USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Australia, or elsewhere — or the reverse. Knowledge of gotra and exact family custom may sit with elders you cannot easily reach.

Common situations

  • Keeping annual observances in India while you cannot travel every year.
  • Adapting observances locally with a priest near you.
  • Aligning siblings across time zones on dates and who will represent the family.
  • Writing down gotra and stories so children inherit more than a vague sense of custom.

How DharmaSetu tries to help

We collect a short picture of what you need, for whom, family region, gotra or sampradaya if known, and where you are based. From that we draft a heritage roadmap and refer you to a verified priest who can confirm what fits. Conversation continues on WhatsApp — not as a self-serve app checkout.

Practical tips (general)

  • Write down any tithi, gotra, or community name elders mention — even fragments help.
  • Ask whether an observance must be in a specific place or can be kept where you live.
  • Prefer clarity over speed: a confirmed annual plan beats an anxious guess.
